Homelab Backups

Privacy and data-use policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Purpose and operator

Homelab Backups is a personal utility operated by its owner to back up the owner's PostgreSQL environments. It is not a public backup service and does not accept third-party customer accounts.

Google user data and permissions

The utility uses Google OAuth to obtain access and refresh tokens with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It creates, lists, uploads, downloads and retires application-created backup files and folders. It does not request full Drive access or intentionally read unrelated pre-existing documents. File names, sizes, identifiers and integrity information are processed only to operate and verify backups.

Backup contents and storage

Archives may contain database records, database-role information, database configuration and recovery credentials belonging to the owner. Contents and names are encrypted on the owner's server before upload. Encryption recovery material is kept separately from the uploaded archives. Google stores the encrypted files and associated service metadata; the utility does not intentionally upload its encryption keys as part of those archives.

Credential handling

OAuth credentials and encryption material are restricted to administrative storage on the owner's systems. They are not intentionally published in source repositories or on this website. They are used only for authorized backup operations.

Use and disclosure

Backup information and Google user data are not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by this utility. Google Drive is used as the storage provider. No additional third-party recipients are configured by this utility.

Retention and deletion

The planned retention is seven daily, four weekly and three monthly recovery points per environment. Older application-created copies are retired only after successful backup processing. Retired Drive files are moved to Drive Trash; Google Drive's trash and account policies govern subsequent deletion. Local recovery copies have separate retention. The owner may delete backup files through Google Drive at any time.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account third-party connections and stop the scheduled job on the server. Revocation prevents future authorized Drive operations but does not automatically delete existing archives. Recovering encrypted archives also requires the separately retained encryption material.

This website

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, application cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary HTTP request information under GitHub's own policies.

Contact and changes

Contact the owner through the project's issue tracker. Do not post secrets, personal database records or backup archives there. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page.
