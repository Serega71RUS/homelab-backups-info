Homelab Backups

A private, owner-operated utility for storing encrypted PostgreSQL recovery copies in the owner's Google Drive.

This is an informational page. It does not provide access to databases, backup archives, credentials or administration interfaces.

What the utility does

  • Creates logical database backup archives on the owner's server.
  • Encrypts archive contents and names before uploading them to Google Drive.
  • Maintains daily, weekly and monthly recovery copies in an application-owned backup folder.
  • Downloads and decrypts copies for integrity and recovery checks.

Google Drive access

The utility requests only the drive.file permission. It manages backup files and folders it creates, rather than requesting access to all existing Drive documents. Only the owner authorizes and operates this installation.

Privacy and data-use policy

Personal infrastructure utility. Updated 6 November 2026. Contact the maintainer — do not include credentials or backup contents in public issues.
